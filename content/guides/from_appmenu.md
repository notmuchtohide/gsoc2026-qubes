{{% steps %}}

### Open the App Menu and select the qube's template

Open the Qubes App Menu and select the TemplateVM where the software should be installed. 

### Click "Add Software"

Click "Add Software" to open the software manager.

![add software](/images/gs_appmenu.png)

### Use the software manager
You can use the software manager to search, install or remove any app that is in the template distribution repositories or Flatpak remotes.

### Shut down the template

### Restart all qubes based on the template

### Add your apps to the App Menu
When you're done, don't forget to add your apps to the App Menu.
You can follow the [documentation](https://doc.qubes-os.org/en/latest/user/troubleshooting/app-menu-shortcut-troubleshooting.html#with-the-graphical-user-interface).
